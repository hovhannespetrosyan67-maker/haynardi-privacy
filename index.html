About HayNardi

HayNardi is an Armenian Long Nardi mobile game for Android. This Privacy Policy explains what information is handled when you use the app, what remains on your device, and how third-party advertising and consent services may process information.

HayNardi has no user accounts or login system, and the developer does not operate a user-profile database. The app currently has no billing or in-app purchases, developer analytics SDK, or developer crash-reporting SDK.

Information stored locally

HayNardi stores game information on your device so the app can function and remember your choices. This may include:

  • language, sound, vibration, and other settings;
  • local economy information, including coins and rewards;
  • daily challenge progress;
  • cosmetic ownership and selections; and
  • other local game state required for functionality.

This local information is not transmitted to developer-operated servers. It remains subject to Android features such as device backup or device transfer where those features are enabled.

Advertising and third-party data processing

HayNardi uses Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK to provide rewarded, interstitial, and banner advertisements. Google and relevant advertising partners may process information such as IP address, device or advertising identifiers, interactions with the app or advertisements, diagnostic information, and device-related information, as applicable.

This information may be used for advertising, ad delivery and measurement, security, and fraud prevention. Advertising-related datasets processed by Google or its partners are not stored by HayNardi in a developer-operated database.

Consent and privacy choices

HayNardi uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and manage consent where legally required. The app gates ad requests using ConsentInformation.canRequestAds(). Where required, users can access privacy options in the app to review or change their consent choices.

Consent handling is provided through UMP; HayNardi does not manually store consent choices in a developer-operated database.

Personalized, non-personalized, or limited advertising

Depending on your location, applicable law, consent choices, and Google's configuration, advertisements may be personalized, non-personalized, limited, or otherwise restricted. The options available to you and the way advertising data is processed may vary accordingly.

Information HayNardi does not request

HayNardi does not request or use your location, contacts, camera, or microphone. The app does not require a user account, login credentials, or a developer-operated personal profile.

Data sharing

HayNardi does not sell users' personal information. The developer does not operate a server database containing personal user profiles. Advertising-related information may be processed or shared by Google and relevant advertising technology partners under their own privacy policies, terms, and legal obligations.

Data retention and deletion

Local HayNardi preferences, game state, and economy information remain on your device until they are changed, cleared or removed through Android settings, or the app is uninstalled. Android backup or device-transfer behavior may apply where enabled.

The developer does not currently maintain a server-side user-account database. Retention and deletion of advertising-related information processed by Google or relevant advertising partners are governed by those parties' policies and legal obligations.

Security

HayNardi minimizes the information handled directly by the developer and uses reasonable safeguards appropriate to the app. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children and target audience

HayNardi's final target-audience and advertising configuration will follow the age groups selected for the app in Google Play Console, applicable Google Play policies, Google Families requirements where applicable, and applicable laws. Advertising and consent behavior may be restricted based on those requirements and the final app configuration.

Third-party services

HayNardi uses the following Google services:

  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK, for rewarded, interstitial, and banner advertisements; and
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP), for consent and privacy choices where required.

Information processed directly by Google is governed by Google's own privacy policies and terms. You can learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and on Google's page about how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes to HayNardi, third-party services, or legal requirements. Any updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
